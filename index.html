Khagrabari: Perfectly Connected Yet Peaceful

Located in Khagrabari, Hamid Residency offers fast connectivity to the railway station, national highway, major hospitals, and central Cooch Behar markets.

Whether you commute daily to the railway hub, central government offices, or regional commercial centers, Khagrabari provides the ideal balance between everyday accessibility and residential tranquility.

New Cooch Behar Junction (NCB)

8 - 12 mins (~3.5 km)

North Bengal’s major railway hub with direct Shatabdi, Rajdhani, Padatik, and Vande Bharat trains to Kolkata, Guwahati, Delhi, and Bangalore.

National Highway 31 (NH-31 / NH-17)

Immediate Access (< 1 km)

Direct arterial highway corridor connecting easily towards Siliguri, Jalpaiguri, Alipurduar, and the Assam border.

Cooch Behar Palace & Town Center

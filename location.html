Locality Guide

Connected Living in Khagrabari

Discover the conveniences of living in Khagrabari, Cooch Behar. Positioned strategically for railway commuters, healthcare workers, and regional professionals.

Railway Connectivity

New Cooch Behar Junction (NCB)

Just 3.5 km away (8 to 12 minutes by Toto or car). Direct high-speed trains to Kolkata (Saraighat, Vande Bharat, Padatik), Delhi (Rajdhani), Guwahati, and Bengaluru. Ideal for transferable railway officials and frequent travelers.

~10 mins commute
Road Arterial

National Highway 31 (NH-31)

Immediate access (< 1 km). Smooth bypass corridor providing quick road travel towards Siliguri, Jalpaiguri, Alipurduar, and the Assam border without dealing with inner town traffic snarls.

< 2 mins to highway
Healthcare Access

MJN Medical College & Hospital

Located roughly 4 km away. Direct access for doctors, medical students, nursing officers, and families who prioritize close proximity to top medical care and specialty clinics.

10-15 mins by Toto
